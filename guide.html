Local Guide

Family adventures in Moonridge and Big Bear Lake.

Where we'd take our own family: snow days, trails, lake time, and a few favorite meals, all near Pine Grove Cabin in upper Moonridge.

Moonridge vs. the Village

Big Bear Lake covers a lot of ground, and Moonridge is its own pocket — up in the pines above the lake, closer to Bear Mountain Resort and the Alpine Zoo than to the restaurants and shops of the Village strip along the water. It's a quieter base: fewer crowds, more forest, and a short drive down to the lake or the Village when you want them. From the cabin it's about 5 minutes to Bear Mountain and the Alpine Zoo, 10 minutes to Snow Summit, and 12 minutes to the Village and Big Bear Lake.

Plan your trip

A perfect family weekend

Not sure where to start? Here are two easy weekends built from our favorites on this page. Mix, match, and leave plenty of time for the hot tub.

Winter weekend

Friday evening. Arrive, settle in, and soak in the hot tub under the string lights. Check Caltrans QuickMap before you drive up.

Saturday. Breakfast at Grizzly Manor Cafe (expect a weekend line), then a day of skiing, snowboarding, or snow play at Bear Mountain. Warm up by the fireplace, then head to 572 Social Kitchen & Lounge (kid and dog friendly) or Saucy Mama's Pizza for dinner.

Sunday. A slow morning with the sleds we keep at the cabin, then the Big Bear Alpine Zoo before the drive home.

Summer weekend

Friday evening. Unload, hot tub, then s'mores at the gas fire pit. Grab the s'mores station from the kitchen.

Saturday. The 2N05 Loop Trail is walkable from the cabin and good for all skill levels. Afternoon at the Alpine Slide at Magic Mountain, then back to fire up the BBQ for dinner on the deck.

Sunday. Breakfast at Grizzly Manor Cafe, a stop at Castle Rock Trail for big views over Big Bear Lake, and the Alpine Zoo on your way out.

Getting outside

Big Bear 2N05 Loop Trail

A 3.2-mile loop within walking distance of the cabin — head up Columbine, turn left on Yosemite to the dead end. Good for all skill levels.

Castle Rock Trail

Moderate difficulty, with standout views over Big Bear Lake.

Bear Mountain & Snow Summit

World-class skiing and snowboarding in winter, mountain biking and scenic lifts in summer.

Big Bear Alpine Zoo

Home to native California wildlife including bears, mountain lions, and birds of prey.

Alpine Slide at Magic Mountain

Alpine slide, mineshaft coaster, and more — a local family favorite.

Action Zipline & Segway Tours

Ziplining and Segway tours in Big Bear Lake.

When to visit

Winter (Dec–Mar)

Skiing and snow play at Bear Mountain. Sleds are provided at the cabin. Roads can require chains — check conditions before driving up.

Summer (Jun–Aug)

Cooler than the valley below, with hiking, mountain biking, and lake access. The cabin has no A/C, but Moonridge evenings rarely need it.

Fall & Spring

The shoulder seasons are quieter and cheaper, with fall color and fewer crowds around the Alpine Zoo and Bear Mountain trails.

Tips from the hosts

A few things we tell every guest

Watch the altitude

Pine Grove Cabin sits at 7,400 ft. Take it easy your first day, especially with hiking or the hot tub — the altitude sneaks up on you. Stay hydrated.

Cell service can be spotty

Download offline maps before heading out for the day.

Evenings cool down fast

Even in summer. Pack a layer for after sunset.

Winter driving

Check Caltrans QuickMap before you drive up — Hwy 18 and Hwy 38 conditions change quickly.
