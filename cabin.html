Kitchen & Dining

Set up for a full group dinner

Gas stove and oven, microwave, drip coffee maker, blender, dishwasher, and a full set of cookware, dishes, and utensils. The dining table seats 6, plus 3 more at the breakfast bar.

Outdoor Living

The deck, from daylight to dusk

The wraparound deck has room for it all: the hot tub, propane BBQ, a gas fire pit with Adirondack chairs, and a dining table, all looking out over the pines. Grab the s'mores station from the kitchen for evenings by the fire.

Good to Know

Practical details

No A/C — central heating & ceiling fans Exterior security cameras Self check-in via keypad Sleeps 7 Driveway parking for 3 vehicles Up to 2 dogs, $75 fee

The cabin sits at 7,400 ft, so summer evenings and winter days both run cooler than you'd expect from Southern California. The cabin relies on central heating rather than A/C — pack layers. The driveway has room for up to 3 vehicles; street parking isn't available.

Family-Friendly

Set up for the youngest guests too

Pack 'n play Kids' toys & books (ages 0–5) Children's dinnerware Outlet covers Children's playroom

A pack 'n play, toys and books for ages 0–5, children's dinnerware, and outlet covers are all on hand. The Sasquatch Den doubles as a children's playroom, with a Lego table and board games alongside the foosball and arcade cabinet.
